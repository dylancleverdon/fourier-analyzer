Projects

    Pull a sound apart, rebuild it in Serum 2.

    1. Drop a section of a track. It is split into stems and drum parts, and the instruments are labelled.
    2. Pick a stem (e.g. the bass), split it into layers if two basses are stacked, and drag over the part you want.
    3. Fourier analysis shows the spectrum, harmonics, notes, envelope, LFO and timing.
    4. Reconstruct searches for a Serum patch (stock waves first), checks it against the original, and exports a .SerumPreset plus MIDI with exact timing.

    These stems came from the basic fallback splitter, not the AI models.
